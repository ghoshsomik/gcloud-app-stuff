Personal Assistant privacy policy

Who uses it. This personal assistant is a personal application operated by its single owner for that owner's own Google account. No other users exist.

What it accesses. With read-only Google permissions it reads Gmail messages and Google Calendar events belonging to the owner. It never sends mail, never creates or edits events, and never accesses other people's data.

Where data goes. Data is processed on hardware the owner controls and is not transmitted to any third party, advertising network, or cloud model provider. Google user data is not sold, shared, or used for any purpose other than answering the owner's own questions.

Retention. Mail and calendar content is fetched on demand and not stored beyond the conversation in which it was requested. The OAuth token is stored on the owner's own hardware and can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.

Limited Use. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact. The owner can be reached at the support address shown on the Google consent screen.
